## What we practise

Security advice should come with **proof**. When we flag a risk, you see the inventory entry it came from, not a severity score we invented. When we say you're covered, you get evidence an auditor will accept. If we can't show you something, it doesn't go in the report.

We're **vendor-neutral by design**. We don't resell products or take referral fees, so an assessment answers one question: what's actually right for your estate. That independence is the point of hiring an outside opinion in the first place.

## What we focus on

Our lead practice is [post-quantum cryptography migration](/services/pqc-migration) — helping organizations find their cryptographic exposure and plan the multi-year transition to the NIST-standardized algorithms before "harvest-now, decrypt-later" becomes a breach. Around it we do the compliance work that regulated enterprises need: [ISO 27001](/services/iso-27001) for information security and [ISO 42001](/services/iso-42001) for AI governance. They overlap more than they look: the cryptographic inventory we build for PQC is also ISO 27001 evidence, so one piece of work often counts twice.

## Who we work with

Regulated and data-sensitive organizations — finance, healthcare, technology, critical infrastructure — across the US, Europe, the UAE and Australia. If your data has to stay confidential for years, or you answer to auditors and regulators, we're built for you.
