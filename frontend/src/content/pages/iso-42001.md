## What we do

We help you establish an AI management system: define the scope and AI policy, run the AI risk and system-impact assessments the standard expects, select and implement the Annex A controls that fit your use cases, and build the Statement of Applicability and evidence base. The goal is governance that stands up to scrutiny — from customers, regulators and your own board — without becoming a tax on every model you ship.

## Why it matters now

AI governance is moving quickly from optional to expected. Customers are adding it to vendor due-diligence, and regulation is arriving. ISO 42001 gives you a recognized framework to show you manage AI responsibly — and pairs naturally with an existing [ISO 27001](/services/iso-27001) ISMS.

## How we work

- **Scoping & AI policy** — what's in the system, and the principles that govern it.
- **Risk & impact assessment** — AI-specific risks and system impacts, assessed against your use cases.
- **Controls & SoA** — the Annex A controls you need, documented and justified.
- **Readiness** — preparation for certification or customer assurance.
