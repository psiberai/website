## Who we are

psiberAI ("we", "us") provides security and AI-governance advisory services. For the purposes of data-protection law, the data controller is [legal entity name], [registered address]. Questions about this policy or your data: [privacy@psiberai.com](mailto:privacy@psiberai.com).

## What we collect

- **Information you send us** — your name, email address, company and any details you include when you contact us or enquire about an assessment.
- **Basic analytics** — aggregate, privacy-preserving usage statistics (page views, referrers) collected without cookies and without tracking you across sites.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use advertising or cross-site tracking.

## How we use it

- To respond to your enquiry and deliver services you request.
- To send you material you have asked for (for example, the newsletter — only with your consent, and you can unsubscribe at any time).
- To understand, in aggregate, how the site is used so we can improve it.

## Lawful basis (UK/EU GDPR)

We rely on your **consent** (for the newsletter), the **performance of a contract** or steps at your request (for enquiries and engagements), and our **legitimate interests** in operating and improving the site, balanced against your rights.

## Sharing

We share personal data only with service providers that help us operate — for example email and hosting providers — under appropriate agreements, and where required by law. Any client information handled during an engagement is governed by the confidentiality terms of that engagement.

## International transfers

We serve clients in the US, Europe, the UAE and Australia. Where personal data is transferred across borders, we use appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) as required.

## Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law, then delete or anonymize it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these, email [privacy@psiberai.com](mailto:privacy@psiberai.com). You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.
